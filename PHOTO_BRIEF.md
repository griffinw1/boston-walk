# Photo hunt: Boston afternoon walk

You're finding better photos for a self-guided walking-tour web page (Boston, a fall afternoon in early November). It's for two visitors, a mom and her daughter. Each stop on the page has one photo. Today's photos are mostly generic shots of buildings. We want photos that show **what you actually do at each stop**: browsing the shelves, holding a cannoli, walking the cobblestones, looking at a grave marker.

## What makes a good photo here

- **People doing the activity.** Shoppers on Newbury, readers in the bookstore, someone crossing the footbridge. Keep it natural, not staged stock poses, and no close-up faces of identifiable strangers as the subject.
- **Fall or winter if possible.** Autumn leaves, bare trees or early dusk light. No spring blossoms or summer crowds in shorts if a good alternative exists.
- **Landscape orientation**, at least **1200 px wide**. The page crops photos to 16:9.
- **Sharp, well lit and not cluttered.** No watermarks, text overlays, collages or heavy filters.
- **Must be the actual place.** Not "a bookstore," but *Beacon Hill Books & Cafe*.

## License: required

The page is public on GitHub Pages, so **only use photos we're allowed to republish**:

1. **Wikimedia Commons** (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, public domain). Best choice.
2. **Unsplash** or **Pexels**. Free license, fine to use.
3. **Flickr** only if the license is CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0. Check the license badge on the photo page and skip "All rights reserved" and "NC/ND" photos.

Do **not** take photos from Google Images, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Instagram, news sites or a business's own website. Record the license and the photographer for every pick.
