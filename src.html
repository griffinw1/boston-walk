Read this first · timing & tips
  • Sunset is about 4:31. Beacon Hill, the Common and most of the Freedom Trail happen in daylight, and you'll reach the North End around dusk. Hanover St is busy and well lit at night.
  • Closing times to know: the Granary Burying Ground closes at 4:00, Paul Revere House at 4:15 and Old North Church at 5:00. At a noon start you'll just make the Granary and Old North if you go straight in, and see Revere's house from outside.
  • Running behind? Skip Copp's Hill first, then the Pinckney St houses. Or take the Green Line from Park Street to Haymarket (2 stops) to jump straight to the North End.
  • Chinatown is an extra at the very end, if you still have energy or want dinner: 3 Orange Line stops from Haymarket. Tap a contactless card or your phone at the gate ($2.40), and bring a little cash for bakeries.
  • Wear good shoes. Acorn St is cobblestone and Beacon Hill's sidewalks are uneven brick.
  • Tap ◎ Me on the map to see where you are. Tap a pin to see a photo of the stop, tap a card to find it on the map, and tap the ✓ to check it off.

Have the best afternoon. — Griffin
