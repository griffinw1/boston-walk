Read this first · timing & tips
  • Sunset is about 4:31. Beacon Hill and the Common happen in daylight. You'll reach Chinatown around dusk and the North End after dark. Hanover St is busy and well lit at night.
  • Paul Revere House closes at 4:15 and Old North Church at 5:00, so at this pace you'll probably see both from outside. If you want to go inside Old North: after the Common, take the Green Line from Park Street to Haymarket (2 stops), do the North End first, then take the Orange Line from Haymarket back to Chinatown for dinner.
  • Running behind? Skip Copp's Hill first, then the Pinckney St houses. To skip Chinatown entirely, take the Green Line from Park Street straight to Haymarket.
  • T fare: tap a contactless credit card or your phone at the gate ($2.40). Bring a little cash for bakeries.
  • Wear good shoes. Acorn St is cobblestone and Beacon Hill's sidewalks are uneven brick.
  • Tap ◎ Me on the map to see where you are. Tap a stop's card to find it on the map, and the ✓ to check it off.
